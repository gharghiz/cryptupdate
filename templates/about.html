About CryptositNews

A real-time crypto intelligence platform that aggregates news from 50+ sources, powered by AI analysis and delivered via website and Telegram bot.

What We Do

CryptositNews collects cryptocurrency, forex, and stock market news from over 50 reliable RSS sources — including CoinTelegraph, Decrypt, CoinDesk, Bloomberg, Forbes, and many more. Every article is processed through our AI pipeline for instant sentiment analysis and summarization.

Our mission is to provide traders and crypto enthusiasts with the fastest, most intelligent news feed available — combining automation, AI, and human-curated source selection.

Key Features

Source Methodology

We carefully select each RSS source based on reliability, speed, and content quality. Sources are categorized into major, crypto-specific, DeFi, security, finance, forex, and Arabic-language feeds. Each source is monitored for failure rates and automatically put on cooldown after repeated errors.

News importance is scored using a multi-layer keyword system that considers breaking alerts, regulatory impact, security incidents, and market-moving events. Only the most impactful news receives AI analysis to optimize API costs while maintaining quality.
