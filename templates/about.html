About CryptositNews

We aggregate, analyze, and deliver cryptocurrency news from 40+ sources in real time, powered by AI and built for traders who need to stay ahead.

Our Mission

Democratizing crypto intelligence

The cryptocurrency market never sleeps, and neither do we. CryptositNews was built to solve a simple problem: crypto news is scattered across dozens of sources, making it nearly impossible to stay informed in real time.

We built an automated system that continuously monitors 40+ news sources, uses AI to analyze and categorize each article, and delivers the most important stories to you the moment they break.

  • Aggregating crypto news from 40+ trusted sources
  • AI-powered analysis and sentiment scoring
  • Real-time delivery via web, Telegram, and SSE
  • Multi-language support (English and Arabic)
