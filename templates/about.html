About {{ site_name }}

The smarter way to follow crypto news

{{ site_name }} is a real-time crypto news aggregator that combines automation, artificial intelligence, and a clean reading experience to help traders stay informed without the noise.

🎯 Our Mission

The crypto space moves fast. Every minute counts. We built {{ site_name }} to filter the noise and deliver only what matters — concise, AI-analyzed news from the most trusted sources in the industry.

⚙️ How It Works

Our system continuously monitors 70+ RSS feeds from top crypto publications including CoinTelegraph, CoinDesk, The Block, Decrypt, and many more. Every 15 minutes, our Python-based scraper fetches the latest articles, runs them through OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini for intelligent summarization, and publishes them to both our website and Telegram channel.

🧠 AI-Powered Analysis

Important and breaking news gets an AI-generated summary, sentiment classification (Bullish/Bearish/Neutral), and a brief reasoning. This gives traders instant context without having to read the full article.

📊 Market Data

Live prices from CoinGecko, TradingView charts, Fear & Greed Index, and comprehensive market tables — all integrated into one clean dashboard.

🏗️ Tech Stack

Backend: Python (Flask, feedparser, OpenAI API)
Database: PostgreSQL / SQLite
Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (dark theme, mobile-first)
APIs: CoinGecko, Alternative.me, TradingView
Hosting: Railway
