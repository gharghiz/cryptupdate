Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Telegram bot free?

Yes! Our Telegram bot is completely free. You can subscribe and receive news alerts, AI summaries, and price updates at no cost.

How often do I receive messages?

You'll receive important breaking news immediately and a curated digest every few hours. The frequency depends on market activity - during high-volatility periods, you may receive more updates.

Can I filter by category?

Absolutely! You can customize which categories you want to receive including Market, DeFi, Regulation, Security, AI, and more. Use the bot's settings menu to manage your preferences.

Does the bot support Arabic?

Yes! The bot fully supports Arabic with proper RTL text formatting. You can switch between English and Arabic at any time using the language command.

How do I set up price alerts?

Simply use the /price command followed by the coin symbol (e.g., /price BTC) to check current prices. You can set alerts with /alert [coin] [price] and the bot will notify you when the target is reached.
